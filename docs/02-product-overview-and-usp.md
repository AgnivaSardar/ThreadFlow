# Product overview and USP

ThreadFlow is a production visibility and shipment-risk tool for knitwear exporters.

## Differentiators
- Explainable, deterministic production run-rate risk.
- Recovery simulator to test the output rate needed to recover a schedule.
- Mobile-first supervisor updates with offline queueing.
- Buyer-safe portal with strict tenant/order isolation.
- Tamil issue-note classification while retaining the original note.
- Audit trail for date changes and human approvals.

## Product principles
Keep the factory workflow extremely short: assigned order, stage, quantity, optional photo/note, submit. Keep risk calculations transparent. Human approval is required before warnings become buyer-visible.
