I built ThreadFlow around the ground realities of Tiruppur's knitwear cluster rather than treating this as an abstract SaaS exercise. Floor supervisors work on low-spec Android phones in tin-roof sheds where Wi-Fi drops constantly, so daily counts queue offline in IndexedDB and sync cleanly without double-counting. Crucially, I avoided using "AI hype" for predicting delays: hallucinated delivery dates destroy trust with factory heads and foreign buyers. Instead, my delay warnings are 100% transparent, auditable math (Sunday-excluded calendar logic, 3-day weighted moving averages, and hard downstream buffer reservations), while AI is reserved strictly for translating spoken Tamil floor notes with an instant, zero-downtime local regex fallback.

I also treated business boundaries as non-negotiable. Foreign buyers have strict confidentiality requirements, so multi-tenant isolation is enforced at the database level—Company B will never see Company C's styles or volumes. Furthermore, unverified AI translations and floor defect photos never reach a buyer's portal automatically; merchandisers review them first in an approval gate. The app is live on Render, backed by 128 automated tests across all core engines, and seeded with the exact sample orders from your brief ready to test using the demo credentials in deployment.md (`ThreadFlow@2026`).
